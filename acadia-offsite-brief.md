## 1. Park Overview

**Acadia National Park** (park code: `acad`) is located on Mount Desert Island in **Bar Harbor, Maine** (Hulls Cove Visitor Center, 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609). It protects the highest rocky headlands along the US Atlantic coastline, with an abundance of habitats and rich cultural heritage. With **~4 million visits/year**, it's one of the top 10 most-visited US national parks.

**Highlights:**
- 27 miles of historic motor roads
- 158 miles of hiking trails
- 45 miles of carriage roads (great for biking/group activities)
- One of the top parks for stargazing and night skies

**Entrance fees (budget reference):**
- Private vehicle: **$35.00** (valid 7 days)
- Motorcycle: $30.00 (7 days)
- Per person (walk/bike in): $20.00 (7 days)
- Nonresident fee: $100.00 per non-US resident 16+ (unless covered by Annual or America the Beautiful pass)
- Timed entry reservation for certain locations: $6.00/vehicle (Cadillac area)

**Getting there:** From Boston, take I-95 north to Augusta, then Route 3 east to Ellsworth and on to Mount Desert Island.

## 3. Current Alerts, Closures & Hazards

*Two active alerts as of last check (2026-11-11). These could change our travel dates, so re-check closer to the trip:*

### ⚠️ Alert 1 — Cadillac Summit Road CLOSED (Nov 9–25 and Dec 1–18)
- **Type:** Park Closure (area inaccessible)
- **Status:** Last updated 11/4/2026
- Cadillac Summit Road is **closed to all users (including bicyclists and pedestrians)** from Nov 9–25 and Dec 1–18 due to construction resuming for an accessible pedestrian path between the East and West parking lots on Cadillac Mountain's summit.
- **Hiking trails on Cadillac Mountain remain open** — so we can still hike up if needed.
- **Additional note:** Park Loop Road will close to motor vehicles for the season **Dec 1** (except access to Jordan Pond via Jordan Pond Road in Seal Harbor, and Sand Beach via Schooner Head Road). If our dates fall in late Nov or December, plan around this seasonal road closure.

### ⚠️ Alert 2 — Eastern Section of Beech Mountain Loop Trail CLOSED
- **Type:** Park Closure (area inaccessible)
- **Status:** Last updated 9/30/2026
- The eastern section of Beech Mountain Loop Trail is closed for repairs (from a junction just beyond the parking lot to just below the summit fire tower).
- **Workaround:** Three alternate routes to the summit are available — the outer loop of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail. A good backup if Beech was on the itinerary.

**Bottom line on alerts:** Neither closure blocks a general trip to Acadia. Cadillac Summit Road is the main vehicle-access issue, and only during the Nov 9–25 / Dec 1–18 window. If our offsite dates fall outside that window (or we plan to hike up Cadillac instead of drive up), these shouldn't derail the trip. Re-check alerts ~1 week before travel.
